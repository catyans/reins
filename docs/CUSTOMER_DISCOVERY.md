# Six-week customer development plan

Owner: founder. Segment: small Agent product teams doing repeatable data collection
and field extraction. No outreach has been sent and no customer agreement is assumed.

## Weeks 1–2: ten problem interviews

Qualify teams with real recurring tasks and access to their API costs and quality
labels. Ask for the last expensive or failed job, how it was detected, which existing
gateway/evaluation tools they use, and who owns the budget. Ask what outcome would
justify a paid trial. Record verbatim evidence rather than inferring willingness to pay.

Interview log columns: team; workload; calls/day; monthly spend (provided by team);
current tools; last incident; manual review effort; desired result; buyer; next step.
Do not collect API keys, raw customer data, or billing credentials in the interview log.

## Weeks 3–4: three design partners

Offer a defined deliverable: integration with one SDK path, a held-out task set,
three strategy comparisons, and a cost/quality report. Each customer uses its own
keys and isolated local database. Agree on the success rubric, billing scope,
report ownership, data handling, and the evaluation API budget before running.
Use the protocol in PILOT.md. Do not promise a percentage saving before measurement.

## Weeks 5–6: first paid pilot

Proposed starting offer for validation: a two-week fixed-fee pilot at RMB 3,000,
customer-funded model usage, one workload and one report. This is a pricing experiment,
not market evidence or an agreed contract. Deliver a recommendation even if no
candidate beats the baseline. Avoid savings-based revenue share until accounting
and a stable baseline are agreed. Discuss a recurring support/policy subscription
only if the customer wants to continue after the trial.

## Continue / narrow / stop criteria

Continue if at least three teams provide real tasks and one pays for the defined
pilot, with ongoing usage and a measurable customer benefit. Narrow the workload
if savings only appear in a subset of tasks. Do not build a broad enterprise
platform merely because developers download a free reporting tool.

## Demo narrative

1. Show the customer task and an accepted result.
2. Show all cost, including failed attempts.
3. Compare strong/small/policy configurations and the quality tradeoff.
4. Show a denied request and a pending charge surviving restart.
5. Ask which measurable outcome matters enough for a paid pilot.
